countries-snapshot.json is generated by scripts/build-countries-snapshot.mjs
from the following sources:

- mledoze/countries (https://github.com/mledoze/countries), licensed under the
  Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: names, ISO codes, region, capital, area,
  languages.
- World Bank, "Population, total" (SP.POP.TOTL), licensed under CC BY 4.0
  (https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL): population figures.
  A small set of territories the World Bank does not report use rounded
  estimates listed in the generator script.
- countries-and-timezones (MIT): IANA time zones, converted to UTC offsets.
- Flag images are loaded at runtime from flagcdn.com.
